About the use of AI

Hey everyone reading this! First off let me introduce myself: my name is Anto, Im 14 and I live in Italy. Ive been into computers and tech ever since I was 5 years old. Right now I use Linux, specifically Arch Linux, which I've been running for almost two years now, and also Debian. I started programming back in 2023, at first with VBScript and Windows Forms, and then I expanded my knowledge into markup languages like HTML and CSS. For the past few months I've been learning Rust and C, and I'm actually managing to do some low-level stuff like writing two kernels, one of which is called Rinux.

A couple of days ago I made a post on Reddit presenting the Rinux project in the r/LinuxItalia subreddit, but all I got back were hateful, accusatory comments about me using AI, some of them were genuinely inhumane and personal. This page exists to explain how I use AI in Rinux and, more generally, in my open-source projects.

How AI is used

To put it briefly: yes, I use AI, but you need to understand what I actually mean by that. I use AI to write complex parts of the code, to write commit descriptions (since I struggle with English and don't really feel like writing commits by hand), and to look for bugs. I can assure you that a lot of Rinux's code was written by me with my current knowledge of Rust. On top of that, the idea and the direction of the project are always guided by me.

Even though I use AI as a helper, Im always the one telling it what to do, what to create or modify and within what limits. I always ask it for a plan of what it's going to implement, and I examine that plan very carefully. And let's remember that Im a solo maintainer.

I think AI is an incredibly useful tool, but it should never replace a human being: there always needs to be human supervision over what AI does.

Disclaimer

Ive already added this line to the project's README, to avoid any misunderstandings for those who care about things like this: 

Disclaimer
----------

Rinux is developed with the help of AI.

Final thoughts

Beyond all this, I personally think that whether a project is made with AI or not, if it works and the creator knows what they're doing in a responsible way, then there's no problem at all, and if you do have a problem with it, just skip it instead of hurting another person. After all, Linux, KDE, GNOME, systemd and another 50,000 open-source projects use AI too.

Honestly Im a pretty friendly person and I dont want to be against anyone, so Im sorry if this is creating so much confusion, it really made me feel awful. I hope this page cleared things up. Thanks for reading!
